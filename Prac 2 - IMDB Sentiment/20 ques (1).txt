🟢 Practical 2: IMDB Sentiment Analysis (DNN Classification)
🎤 20 Viva Questions + Answers

What is the objective of this practical?
👉 The objective is to classify movie reviews as positive or negative using deep learning.

What type of problem is this?
👉 This is a binary classification problem.

What is the IMDB dataset?
👉 It is a dataset of movie reviews labeled as positive or negative.

How is text represented in this dataset?
👉 Text is converted into sequences of numbers representing word indexes.

Why do we use get_word_index()?
👉 It maps words to integers so that the model can process text data.

What is padding and why is it used?
👉 Padding ensures all sequences are of equal length for uniform input to the model.

What is an Embedding layer?
👉 It converts words into dense vector representations for better learning.

Why do we use a Sequential model here?
👉 Sequential model is simple and suitable for stacking layers in order.

Why is sigmoid used in output layer?
👉 Sigmoid produces output between 0 and 1, suitable for binary classification.

What is binary crossentropy?
👉 It is a loss function used to measure error in binary classification tasks.

What is the role of optimizer?
👉 It adjusts weights to minimize the loss during training.

What is accuracy?
👉 Accuracy is the percentage of correct predictions made by the model.

What does model.fit() do?
👉 It trains the model using training data over multiple epochs.

Why is preprocessing required for text?
👉 Because neural networks cannot understand raw text directly.

What is tokenization?
👉 Tokenization converts words into numerical form.

What is overfitting in this model?
👉 When the model performs well on training data but poorly on test data.

What does model.evaluate() do?
👉 It checks model performance on test data.

What does model.predict() do?
👉 It predicts whether a review is positive or negative.

Why deep learning for NLP?
👉 Deep learning captures complex patterns in language data.

What improvement can you make in this model?
👉 We can add more layers or use LSTM for better performance.
